# 1 | | Me and My Family | 21 | 28
@area Listening
- [l-disc] Identify and discriminate sounds.
- [l-same] Identify words that have the same initial, medial or final sound.
- [l-rhyme] Recognize, identify and produce rhyming words (e.g. cow → now).
- [l-indiv] Identify individual sounds in words.
- [l-comp] Comprehend words and simple expressions.
- [l-resp] Respond to the audio or the teacher verbally and non-verbally.
- [l-key] Identify key information from a short speech or conversation.
- [l-follow] Follow the message in short simple conversations.
- [l-tasks] Perform a variety of listening comprehension tasks.
@area Speaking
- [s-decode] Decode phonetically regular and irregular words using letter sound knowledge, e.g. rat, too, blank, house, have, said, where, etc.
- [s-produce] Produce words, phrases, simple sentences with intelligible pronunciation.
- [s-conv] Participate in short conversation with teachers and friends using simple English.
- [s-ask] Ask and answer short, simple questions.
- [s-sing] Sing or recite a song/chant by listening to the teacher or an audio.
@area Reading
- [r-sounds] Put sounds together to read words and sentences.
- [r-visual] Recognize and read words with the help of the visuals.
- [r-meaning] Understand the meaning of grade appropriate words.
- [r-rhymes] Perform rhymes and chants with appropriate rhyme and rhythm.
- [r-sent] Read sentences and short paragraphs correctly.
- [r-info] Retrieve specific information (names, number, etc.) from reading texts.
- [r-decode] Decode words correctly which have consonant diagraphs in initial positions (th, sh, ch, wh), vowel diagraphs (oo, ee, ea, oa, aw, ai, ay), initial consonant blends (sw, sn, sk, bl, br, ch, cl, cr, dr, fl, gl, ph, pl, pr, sh, sl, sm, sp, st, th, tr, tw, wh, qu), and final consonant blends (nd, nk, nt, mp).
@area Writing
- [w-order] Write words in correct order to form simple sentences.
- [w-dict] Write dictated words, phrases and sentences.
- [w-caps] Use capital letters, full stops and question marks correctly.
- [w-personal] Give basic personal information in writing (e.g. name, address, age, nationality, family information, etc.).
- [w-spell] Use correct spelling of words.
- [w-coord] Connect sentences using simple coordinators (e.g. 'and' and 'but').
- [w-hand] Demonstrate good handwriting skills.
