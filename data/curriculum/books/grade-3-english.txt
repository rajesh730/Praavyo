# 1 | | Me and My Family | 18 | 26
@area Listening
- [l-rhyme] Recognize, identify and produce rhyming words. e.g. near → here.
- [l-resp] Listen and respond to a wide variety of spoken stimuli (words, sentences, questions, instructions, etc.) both verbally and non-verbally.
- [l-follow] Follow the message in short simple conversations.
- [l-key] Identify key information from a short speech or conversation.
- [l-find] Find out specific information (names, dates, time, etc) from different kinds of simple spoken language.
- [l-tasks] Perform a variety of listening comprehension tasks.
- [l-songs] Listen and comprehend a range of songs and stories with a variety of visual clues.
@area Speaking
- [s-produce] Produce words, phrases, simple sentences with intelligible pronunciation.
- [s-conv] Participate in short conversations.
- [s-sing] Sing or recite a song/chant by listening to the teacher or an audio.
@area Reading
- [r-silent] Recognise silent letters, e.g. e (home), b (comb), k (knee), gh (right), w (write), t (often), l (walk), etc.
- [r-multi] Read multi-syllabic words composed of roots, prefixes, and suffixes.
- [r-abbr] Read common abbreviations (e.g., Dr., Mr., AM, and PM).
- [r-rhymes] Perform rhymes and chants with appropriate rhyme and rhythm.
- [r-retrieve] Read short texts silently and retrieve specific information (name, number, etc.).
- [r-guess] Guess the meaning of unfamiliar words from contexts.
- [r-dict] Consult dictionary to find out the meaning of words.
- [r-para] Retrieve required information from para-orthographic texts such as charts, tables, graphs and maps.
- [r-poems] Read poems and short stories for pleasure.
@area Writing
- [w-caps] Use capital letters, full stops and question marks correctly.
- [w-coord] Connect sentences using simple coordinators (e.g. and, but, because).
- [w-para] Write in a simple paragraph about oneself, people, places and things.
- [w-spell] Use correct spelling of words.
- [w-verbs] Use present (with/without -s/es) and regular past (-ed) forms of verbs
- [w-mean] Use words in meaningful sentences.
- [w-hand] Demonstrate good handwriting skills.
